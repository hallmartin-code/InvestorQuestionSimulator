## Data status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `VERIFIED_FROM_DECK` | Stated in the deck; the slide and a verbatim quote are recorded. |
| `USER_PROVIDED` | Entered or corrected by the founder; takes precedence over the deck. |
| `CALCULATED` | Derived by a deterministic formula from other fields; shown with a (calculated) suffix. |
| `INFERRED` | Interpretation, allowed only for categorical fields; shown with an (inferred) suffix. |
| `MISSING` | Not available; shown as the missing-value marker. |

## Claim type vocabulary

| Claim type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `FACT` | A deck statement carrying a third-party citation. |
| `FOUNDER_CLAIM` | Asserted by the deck or the founder without independent support. |
| `ASSUMPTION` | A projection, target, plan or use of funds. |
| `CALCULATION` | Arithmetic on stated figures. |
| `INFERENCE` | An interpretation, never presented as fact. |
| `MISSING` | Information that is not available. |

## Analysis rules

1. Every value comes from the session: the deck-derived company profile, founder inputs, interview responses and deterministic calculations. No outside data is added.
2. Scores, percentages, coverage, classifications and snapshot values are calculated in Python; the model never calculates report values.
3. Missing information is printed as the missing-value marker and is never estimated.
4. Calculated and inferred snapshot values carry a (calculated) or (inferred) suffix; founder claims are never presented as verified facts.
5. Narrative text (assessment, concerns, next steps, practice questions) may be model-written, but every figure must trace to the deck, founder inputs or answers; any other figure is replaced with a bracketed placeholder.
6. Categories not covered by the interview are shown as not assessed and excluded from the overall score; the score is marked provisional when coverage is below 60% of the weighting or fewer than five answers were given.
7. The report must fit one page: the layout is rebuilt down the fit ladder until it does.
